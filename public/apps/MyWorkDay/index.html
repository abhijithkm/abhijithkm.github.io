MyWorkDay

A personal developer workday cockpit — start your day, let it quietly observe your Git activity, Jira, Bitbucket, and Calendar, then consolidate what actually happened.

What it is

MyWorkDay pulls today's context together on your own machine — no cloud backend, no accounts, no telemetry. It is not a Jira replacement, a team dashboard, or a productivity-scoring tool. It is a single-user desktop app that helps you plan a day, quietly gathers evidence of what you actually worked on, and turns that into a factual, human-reviewed daily record.

The workday lifecycle

Start→ Plan→ Observe→ Review→ Consolidate→ History

What it connects to

Local Git

Observed activity estimated from real commit history — the source of truth for what you actually worked on.

Jira

Your assigned tickets, read-only. Never posts worklogs or changes status.

Bitbucket

Pull requests you're involved in, read-only via OAuth.

Google Calendar

Today's meetings, read-only, so your plan accounts for them.

Google Chat

Optional — view and set your own availability status, only when you ask it to.

Principles

  • Everything stays on your own computer
  • No productivity scores, no ranking, no gamification
  • Evidence and interpretation stay distinguishable
  • You confirm the plan and the final record — nothing is finalized automatically
  • One integration failing never blocks the others
